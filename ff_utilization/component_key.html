What do these stats mean?

Snaps, Rush, I5, Targets, Rec, EZ and ADOT (and every % built from them) come straight from the NFL's own official play-by-play and snap-count data, not a charter's judgment call. Routes and Rte% are the one exception -- there is no official, public route-participation feed, so those two are charted by FantasyLife.

Snaps / Snap%
Offensive snaps played, and the share of the team's offensive snaps that represents.
Rush / Rush%
Rush attempts, and share of the team's rush attempts.
I5 / I5%
Rush attempts from inside the opponent's 5-yard line ("goal-line carries"), and share of the team's goal-line carries.
Routes / Rte%
Routes run, and share of the team's pass-play routes. Charted, not official -- see note above.
Tgts / Tgt%
Targets, and share of the team's targets.
Rec
Receptions.
EZ / EZ%
Targets thrown into the end zone, and share of the team's end zone targets.
ADOT
Average depth of target -- how far downfield, in the air, the player's targets traveled on average.

Weekly shows one row per player per selected week. Season adds every selected week together for each player, then recalculates each percentage from those combined totals (never averages the weekly percentages). The full member table's Season view also has a Total / Per Game toggle -- Per Game divides the counting stats (not the percentages) by games played.

Free previews show Snaps, Rush, Targets and Rec only -- every other column above requires a membership.
