Streaming a defense every week is one of those lineup decisions that never really gets easier — one week you're chasing a plus matchup against a rookie QB, the next you're trying to figure out if a top-10 unit on paper is actually worth trusting on the road. The Fantasy Football Defense Stream-O-Matic takes the guesswork out of it by pulling together the matchup factors that actually matter — Vegas game scripts, pressure rates, DVOA, expert consensus, and more — into one weekly-updated chart, so you can see at a glance which defenses are set up to produce and which ones you should leave on your bench. Below is the key explaining exactly how the chart is built and scored, including links to every source behind it, if you want to dig into the "why" behind any team's ranking.

How the Chart Works

Scoring

Point totals are assigned to each team using a traditional rotisserie scoring style commonly used in fantasy baseball season-long leagues.

Categories

The categories for the chart are listed below, along with links to websites I use to find this data.
  • Rost%/Start%: Roster percentages based on Yahoo Fantasy Football leagues. This gives us an idea of who is available. This doesn't factor into the standings.
  • Location (Home): Defenses generally play better at home than on the road. All home teams receive a five-point bonus.
  • Vegas (Opp Implied): Using Vegas odds, pulled from odds-api. The spread and over/under generate an implied points total for each team. This game score portrays what the game flow could look like and, therefore, which fantasy football defenses are in a good position to succeed.
  • Pressure (Opp Prss%): Thanks to Sharp Football, we have access to advanced passing statistics. I focused on pressure percentages allowed here, which includes QB hurries, hits, and sacks.
  • DVOA: Probably the most popular tool for evaluating defenses, the DVOA efficiency ratings are utilized to give a strong baseline to the rankings. FTN Fantasy has DAVE DVOA that I am using. Use these ratings early in the year to avoid overreactions.
  • ECR: The team that is ranked #1 on FantasyPros ECR for the week gets the maximum points for the week.
  • Proj: Using Yahoo! projections for this column. It is added as a bonus and not scored like roto for all the other columns.
